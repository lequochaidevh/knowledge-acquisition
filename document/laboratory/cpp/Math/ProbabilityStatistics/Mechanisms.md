### Basic Probability and Bayes' Theorem

**Conditional Probability:** The probability of an event occurring given that another event has already occurred.

**Bayes' Theorem:** The ultimate formula to update the probability of a hypothesis when encountering new data. This is the foundation of classification algorithms (like Naive Bayes for spam filtering.

### Probability Distributions and Summary Statistics

**Summary Statistics:** Mean, Median, Variance, and Standard Deviation to measure the central tendency and spread of a dataset.

**Gaussian / Normal Distribution:** The classic bell-shaped curve found everywhere. ML models often assume features follow this distribution for optimal mathematical convergence.
